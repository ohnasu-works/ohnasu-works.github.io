Glow Mirror Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

1. Our approach

Glow Mirror (“the App”) never sends camera video, photos or videos to the developer or to any outside server. The developer does not collect or store your personal information.

2. Data the App handles and where it's kept

  • Camera video: used only to show you on screen as a mirror. It never leaves your device
  • Freeze frames and 10-second videos: kept temporarily on your device for review and deleted when you close the review screen. They are saved to Photos only when you tap Save
  • Photos added to Compare (Before & After, Flipbook): kept in memory only while the App is running and discarded when it closes
  • Settings (button layout, launch state and so on): stored only on your device

3. Why the App asks for permissions

  • Camera: to show you as a mirror. Capture happens only when you tap a button
  • Add to Photos: to save the freeze frames, videos and Before & After images you choose to save
  • Choosing photos: to pick photos for Compare. The App uses Apple's photo picker and receives only the photos you select (it does not access your whole library)

4. Advertising

To stay free, the App uses Google AdMob, an advertising service provided by Google. To show ads, Google may collect information such as device identifiers (for example an advertising ID), approximate location (estimated from your IP address), and how ads are displayed and tapped. This information is handled under Google's privacy policy, and the developer does not use it to identify you.

  • The App never passes camera video, photos or videos to any advertising service
  • The App does not ask for “App Tracking” permission and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites (it does not use the IDFA)
  • In regions where consent is required, such as the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, a consent screen appears before ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the App under Settings › Ad Privacy Choices
  • Purchasing “Remove Ads” stops all ads
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

5. Other third-party services

  • App Store / StoreKit: to purchase and restore “Remove Ads” (payment information is handled by Apple; the developer does not receive it)
  • The App does not use any analytics service

6. Deleting your data

Deleting the App removes its settings and temporary files from your device. Images and videos you saved to Photos can be deleted in the Photos app.

7. Children's privacy

The App is not directed primarily at children under 13. The developer does not collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the update on this page.
