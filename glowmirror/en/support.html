FAQ

Is it free?

Every feature is free to use. The app shows ads, and the one-time “Remove Ads” purchase removes them all.

How do I restore my purchase?

Tap Tools › Settings › Restore Purchases. If you bought it with the same Apple Account, ads stay removed on your new device too.

The mirror doesn't appear

Camera access may be turned off. Open the Settings app › Glow Mirror › Camera and turn it on. If another app is using the camera, close it and try again.

Are photos or videos saved automatically?

No. They're saved to Photos only when you tap Save. Closing the review screen deletes them, so your storage never fills up.

I can't save to Photos

Open the Settings app › Glow Mirror › Photos and choose “Add Photos Only” or “Full Access”.

What does Flip do?

A normal mirror shows you reversed left to right. Flip shows your face the way other people see you (the same way it looks in photos).

The light doesn't seem brighter

Light sets the screen to maximum brightness, and on supported iPhones makes it glow even brighter. The change is smaller if your screen is already bright or you're outdoors. If your face looks dark, also try Tools › Brightness.

I lost track of what I turned on

Tap Tools › Reset. Flip, Light, Guide, Ghost overlay, Zoom, Brightness and Lighting all go back to how they are when the app starts.

Can I change the bottom buttons?

Yes. In Tools › Settings › Bottom Buttons, choose which features appear next to the Freeze button. You can also choose to start flipped or with the light on.

How do I check the back of my hair?

Tap “10s Video” and slowly turn your head to the side and back. When recording ends, the review screen opens so you can watch in slow motion or frame by frame.

Which languages are supported?

Japanese, English, Korean, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Thai, Vietnamese and Indonesian. The app follows your iPhone's language. You can also change it in the Settings app › Glow Mirror › Language.
