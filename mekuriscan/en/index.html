Screens

Scan, shelve, enjoy. All in one app.

01 · Trading cards

Trading cards: front and back as one.

Place a card in the frame and it scans automatically. Flip it over and the front and back become one card. Cropping, perspective correction and glare removal are automatic.

  • Organize idol photocards, sports cards and game cards into folders
  • Record condition, date acquired and notes. Zoom in to check the corners
  • Create a front-and-back image to share on social media or marketplaces
  • Enjoy your collection as a slideshow in viewer mode

02 · Manga · Magazines

Manga and magazines: turn the page, build a book.

Turn the page and hold still — it scans automatically. Spreads are split into single pages and saved as one book on your bookshelf.

  • Page curve correction, finger removal and even lighting for clean pages
  • Title and author filled in from the cover or barcode
  • Retake, insert or reorder any single page later
  • Import PDFs, images, folders, ZIP and RAR archives

FAQ

FAQ

Where are my scans saved?

Only on your device. With Pro automatic backup, they are also saved to the cloud folder you choose (iCloud Drive and others).

Does it work on Mac?

Yes, on Macs with Apple silicon (M1 or later). You can scan with the built-in camera or Continuity Camera, or drag and drop PDFs and images from your scanner.

Do I need to cut my books apart?

No. Just turn the pages of an open book. Page curves and fingers in the shot are corrected automatically.

Can I share scanned books with others?

Mekuri Scan is for organizing the cards and books you own. Please do not distribute or publish copyrighted pages.
