Mekuri Scan Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 28, 2026

1. Our approach

Mekuri Scan ("the app") does not send your scanned images or the card and book information you enter to the developer or any outside server. The developer does not collect or store your personal information.

2. Data the app handles and where it is stored

  • Scanned and imported images (card fronts and backs, book pages): stored only on your device
  • Card and book information (names, folders, tags, condition, notes, date acquired, purchase price, titles, authors, bookmarks, etc.): stored only on your device
  • Page text: read with on-device text recognition (Apple Vision) so you can search inside books, and stored only on your device
  • Face matching: to suggest card names, facial features are compared on your device only and never sent anywhere
  • Settings: stored only on your device

3. Information sent outside (only when looking up a book's title and author)

Only when filling in a book's title and author from its cover or barcode, the barcode number (ISBN) or the title text read from the cover is sent to the following services to search. Images are never sent. You can turn this off in Settings › Book Scanning.

  • National Diet Library Search (https://ndlsearch.ndl.go.jp/)
  • Google Books API (Google Privacy Policy)

4. Permissions

  • Camera: to scan and save cards and books. The video never leaves your device
  • Add to Photos: to save an image to Photos when you choose "Save Image" in the share menu
  • Photo and file pickers: to import books and cards from photos or files. The app only receives the items you choose
  • App Tracking: to show more relevant ads in the free version. All features work even if you do not allow it

5. Advertising

The free version uses Google AdMob, an advertising service provided by Google. To show ads, Google may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising identifier), approximate location (estimated from the IP address), ad impressions and taps, and information about how the app runs. This information is handled under Google's Privacy Policy, and the developer cannot identify you from it.

  • The app never passes your scanned images or card and book information to the ad service
  • The advertising identifier is used only if you allow App Tracking. You can change this anytime in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking
  • In regions that require consent, such as the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, a consent form is shown before ads. You can change your choice in the app's Settings › Ad consent settings
  • Purchasing Mekuri Scan Pro removes all ads
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from partner apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

6. Backup

If you use the Pro automatic backup, data is saved only to the location you choose (iCloud Drive, Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox or other locations available in the Files app). The storage provider's privacy policy applies there. The developer never sees the contents of your backups.

7. Other third-party services

  • App Store / StoreKit: purchasing and restoring Mekuri Scan Pro (payment information is handled by Apple and never received by the developer)
  • No analytics services are used

8. Deleting your data

Deleting a card or book in the app deletes its images and information from your device. Deleting the app deletes all its data on your device (backups you saved yourself and images saved to Photos remain).

9. Children's privacy

The app is not primarily directed at children under 13. The developer does not collect children's personal information.

10. Changes to this policy

Any changes will be posted on this page.
