Mekuri Scan Support

If something is not working, please check the FAQ below first. If that does not help, email us with your device model, OS version, and what you did on which screen.

FAQ

I want to save a card without its back

Tap "Front only" on the scan screen to save just the front.

Auto-capture does not trigger

Put the card or page inside the frame and hold your phone still for a moment. Good light and an angle without ceiling-light glare help. You can also scan manually with the shutter button.

Spreads are paired one page off

Tap "Shift by one page" in the reader's top menu. Books where you scanned only the cover or skipped a blank page will then pair correctly.

I want to retake a single page

In the book details, long-press a page and choose Retake. You can also insert, delete and reorder pages.

Moving to a new device

Choose a location for Pro automatic cloud backup, then restore it from Settings on your new device.

I bought Pro but still see ads

Tap "Restore Purchases" in Settings. With the same Apple Account, Pro works on all your iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Importing scanner PDFs on Mac

Drag and drop PDFs, images, folders or ZIP files onto the bookshelf. You can also press ⌘O to choose files.
