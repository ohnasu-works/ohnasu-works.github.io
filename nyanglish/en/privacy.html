Nyanglish Privacy Policy

Effective: November 16, 2026 / Provider: Oh!nasu Works

Oh!nasu Works ("we") sets out below how user information is handled in the app "Nyanglish" for iOS, iPadOS and macOS (the "App").

1. Information we collect

The App has no account or sign-in. We do not collect personal information such as your name or email address, and nothing is sent to our servers. However, the free version shows ads, so the ad provider (Google) receives device information as described in "6. Ads and tracking" below.

2. Data stored on your device

  • Game progress (cleared stages, scores, picture book, outfits, items)
  • Study records (word-book progress, review of weak points, daily stamps, mock test results, etc.)
  • Settings such as volume, answer mode, language and handedness

This data is stored on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, game progress is also saved to your own iCloud (an Apple service) so you can continue on other devices with the same Apple Account. We never receive or see this data.

3. Microphone and speech recognition

The pronunciation challenge, the weak-sound practice and the game's voice-answer mode use the microphone and Apple's speech recognition. The microphone is used only from when you tap the microphone button until you finish speaking. Speech is recognized on the device whenever possible; recordings are never saved or sent to us. If your device does not support on-device recognition, Apple's speech recognition service may process it under Apple's privacy policy. You can change microphone and speech recognition permissions later in the Settings app.

4. Read-aloud audio

English sentences are read aloud using audio bundled in the App and the device's speech feature. No information is sent outside for this.

5. Payments

Premium (one-time purchase) is bought through Apple's App Store. We never receive your payment information.

6. Ads and tracking

In the free version, ads are shown in the game and study screens using Google AdMob, an ad service provided by Google LLC (no ads after buying Premium). To show ads, measure their performance and prevent fraud, AdMob may automatically collect information such as:

  • The advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow it), device and OS type, language and other device information
  • Approximate location estimated from your IP address
  • Records of ad views and taps, and information about app performance and crashes

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the App asks for your consent through Google's consent message before showing ads; you can change your choice at any time from "Ad privacy settings" in the App's settings. The App may also ask, through Apple's dialog, for permission to track you across apps. Every feature works even if you do not allow it (you will then see general ads that are not tailored to you).

AdMob's handling of information follows How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

7. Children

We do not collect personal information from users. Ads in the free version are limited to content suitable for all ages. For Premium purchases, we recommend parents use Apple's Family Sharing and Ask to Buy.

8. Changes

We may update this policy as needed. Any changes will be posted on this page.
