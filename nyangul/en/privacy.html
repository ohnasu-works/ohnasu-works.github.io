Nyangul Privacy Policy

Effective: November 1, 2026 (revised regarding ads) / Provider: Oh!nasu Works

Oh!nasu Works ("we," "us" or "our") sets out below how user information is handled in "Nyangul," our iOS / iPadOS app (the "App").

1. Information we collect

The App has no account registration or login. We do not collect personal information such as your name or email address, and we do not send any such information to our servers. However, because the free version displays ads, our advertising provider (Google) receives information from your device, as described in "7. Ads and tracking" below.

2. Data stored only on your device

  • Learning records (XP, stage medals, word mastery, streaks, affection levels and so on)
  • Game progress such as trading cards, coins and outfits
  • The name, birthday and display settings you set
  • Photos you choose in Oshi photo settings (icon, wallpaper, home screen art, clear screen)

This data is stored on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, your learning and game records (excluding photos) are also saved to your own iCloud account (an Apple service) so you can continue on any iPhone or iPad using the same Apple Account. We never receive or see this data. You can stop saving to iCloud by turning off this app under Settings › [your name] › iCloud on your iPhone.

3. Access to photos

In Oshi photo settings, the App loads only the photos you select. It does not read your entire photo library, and it never sends photos anywhere outside your device (including iCloud). Photos are stored only on that device.

4. Microphone and speech recognition

Conversation mode, Chat with your oshi and the Video-Call Pronunciation Check use the microphone and Apple's speech recognition to score the Korean you speak. We never receive your recorded audio. Speech recognition is a feature provided by Apple and, depending on your device, may be processed on Apple's servers. Such processing is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. You can turn off microphone and speech recognition access at any time in your iPhone's Settings.

5. Game Center

The leaderboard feature uses Apple's Game Center. If you are signed in, your game scores are sent to Game Center. Game Center information is managed by Apple, and we do not receive your Apple Account information. You can play the App without signing in.

6. Payments

Premium (a one-time purchase) is purchased through Apple's App Store. We never receive your payment information.

7. Ads and tracking

The free version displays ads using Google AdMob, an advertising service provided by Google LLC (ads are not shown once you purchase Premium). To display ads, measure their performance and prevent fraud, AdMob may automatically collect information such as the following:

  • Device information such as the advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow it), device and OS type, and language
  • Approximate location estimated from your IP address
  • Records of ad impressions and taps, and information about app performance and crashes

When you first launch the App (or at a similar time), Apple's prompt will ask for your permission to track you across apps. Even if you don't allow tracking, you can use every feature (in that case, you'll see general, non-personalized ads). You can change this later under Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking on your iPhone.

AdMob's handling of information is governed by How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

8. Use by children

We do not collect users' personal information. Ads in the free version are limited to content suitable for all audiences. For Premium purchases, we recommend that parents or guardians set up Apple's Family Sharing and Ask to Buy.

9. Changes

We may revise this policy as needed. If we do, we will post the update on this page.
