Frequently asked questions

Is it free to play?

The Intro through Pre-Intermediate courses, Levels 1–3 of the mini games, the dictionary, Lessons 1 and 2 of the textbook and more are free to play. To unlock every feature, purchase Premium (a one-time purchase).

How much does Premium cost?

Premium is a one-time purchase. To celebrate the launch, it's 30% off until February 28, 2027. The price varies by country/region, so please check the App Store for the price where you live. It's not a subscription, so you'll never be charged automatically.

How do I restore my purchase?

Tap "Restore Purchases" on the purchase screen in the app. As long as you use the same Apple Account, Premium carries over after switching devices or reinstalling the app.

The microphone isn't working / I can't answer by voice

On your iPhone, go to Settings › Nyangul and make sure both Microphone and Speech Recognition are turned on. Recognition may not work well if another app is recording or if Bluetooth earphones are connected.

I want to type with a Korean keyboard

You can type with the Korean keyboard built into the app's screens. If you'd rather use the iPhone's Korean keyboard, add it under Settings › General › Keyboard › Keyboards › Add New Keyboard › Korean. You'll then be able to switch to it with the button below the input field.

Can I continue on both my iPhone and iPad?

Yes. If you're signed in to iCloud with the same Apple Account, your learning progress, trading cards, coins, outfits, story progress and more sync automatically. Oshi photos are set separately on each device. To use Premium on your other device, tap "Restore Purchases" there.

What happens to my progress when I get a new device?

If you're signed in to iCloud, your progress comes back when you open the app on your new device. Restoring from an iPhone backup also carries it over. Your Premium purchase can be brought back with "Restore Purchases."

Are the idols in the app real?

No. All idols and characters in the app are fictional.

Are these official TOPIK questions?

No. The TOPIK-style questions and estimated levels are original to this app and are not affiliated with the official exam.
