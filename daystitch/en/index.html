Features

Everything for your day, on one page.

A calendar that's easy to read

  • Year, month, week (24-hour vertical) and day views
  • Type “Lunch tomorrow 12pm” and it becomes an event
  • Show events from the Calendar app, including Google and Outlook calendars
  • Events that span several days appear as one bar across the month
  • Weather with highs and lows, 10 days ahead and for past days too

To-dos without the clutter

  • Just three lists: Today, Upcoming and Someday
  • Checklists inside a to-do, repeats, due dates and reminders
  • On the due date, a to-do moves to Today with a red flag
  • Let AI tidy up today’s list: related to-dos are grouped together, and you can undo it anytime

Record meetings, get minutes

  • Tap the mic on a day page and keep writing while it records
  • Transcribed on your device, then AI writes key points, minutes and action items
  • Pick a minutes template (team meeting, client meeting and more) or make your own
  • Send action items to your to-dos and search the full transcript later

A handwritten notes app, too

  • Write with Apple Pencil or your finger. The page grows as you write
  • One-tap eraser, photo cropping, stickers and washi tape. Long-press to move anything
  • Switch to Notes only mode to use it as a dated handwritten notes app, without events or to-dos
  • Handwriting is recognized on device, so you can search it

Habits, widgets, Apple Watch and Siri

  • Check off habits like “Morning medicine” with one tap and watch your streak grow
  • Home Screen widgets: two-week and two-month calendars, calendar with to-dos, and more
  • See and complete today’s plans on your wrist. Changes on iPad show up on iPhone and Apple Watch right away

Private by design

  • Your data stays on your device and in your iCloud. The developer can’t see it
  • No ads and no tracking
