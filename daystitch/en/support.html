Frequently asked questions

Can I use it for free?

Without a purchase, you can still view, search and export your data. To create or edit, choose Lifetime (a one-time purchase) or the AI Minutes plan. New AI Minutes subscribers get 2 weeks free (up to 60 minutes of AI minutes in total during the trial). Cancel during the trial and you won’t be charged.

How much does it cost?

Lifetime unlocks every feature except AI minutes, forever, at a launch price through February 28, 2027. The AI Minutes plan (monthly or yearly) adds up to 900 minutes of AI minutes per month, and Lifetime owners get a discount. Prices are shown in the App and on the App Store, and vary by region.

How do I cancel?

Open the Settings app on your iPhone and go to Apple Account › Subscriptions › Daystitch.

How do I restore my purchase?

In the App, go to Settings › Plan › Restore Purchases.

What happens to my data when I get a new phone?

If iCloud sync is on, your data comes back automatically on a new device signed in with the same Apple Account. You can also export a backup manually from Settings › Backup.

Which languages are supported?

English and Japanese. The App follows your device language. To change it for Daystitch only, open the Settings app › Apps › Daystitch › Language.

AI minutes aren’t created

AI minutes are part of the AI Minutes plan. With the plan, minutes are created automatically when you stop recording (if auto-summary is on in Settings › Recording). An internet connection is required. You can also create them from the recording card.

Does transcription cost extra?

On-device transcription is included with Lifetime at no extra cost. AI minutes are part of the AI Minutes plan, up to 900 minutes per month.

What can I do on Apple Watch?

See today’s and tomorrow’s events and today’s to-dos, complete to-dos, add to-dos by voice, and show your next event on your watch face.

My widget is empty

Open Daystitch once, then go back to the Home Screen and the widget will update. If it still doesn’t show anything, touch and hold the widget to remove it, then add it again.
