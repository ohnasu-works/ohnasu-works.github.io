Frequently asked questions

Can I use it for free?

You can use every feature free for the first week. After that, Daystitch is an annual or monthly subscription. If you cancel during the free week, you won’t be charged.

How much does it cost?

Prices are shown in the App and on the App Store, and vary by region. Launch pricing applies to sign-ups through February 28, 2027. From March 1, 2027, the price for new subscribers will go up, and anyone who signed up at the launch price keeps that price.

How do I cancel?

Open the Settings app on your iPhone and go to [your name] › Subscriptions › Daystitch.

How do I restore my purchase?

In the App, go to Settings › Plan › Restore Purchases.

What happens to my data when I get a new phone?

If iCloud sync is on, your data comes back automatically on a new device signed in with the same Apple Account. You can also export a backup manually from Settings › Backup.

Which languages are supported?

English and Japanese. The App follows your device language. To change it for Daystitch only, open the Settings app › Apps › Daystitch › Language.

I don’t see the AI features

AI features work on devices that support Apple Intelligence (such as iPhone 15 Pro or later and iPad with M1 or later) on iOS 26 or later, with Apple Intelligence & Siri turned on in Settings. On other devices, you can use them by adding your own ChatGPT or Gemini API key.

Does transcription cost extra?

On-device transcription and AI summaries are included at no extra cost. Only if you choose ChatGPT or Gemini will that company bill you directly for usage.

What can I do on Apple Watch?

See today’s and tomorrow’s events and today’s to-dos, complete to-dos, add to-dos by voice, and show your next event on your watch face.

My widget is empty

Open Daystitch once, then go back to the Home Screen and the widget will update. If it still doesn’t show anything, touch and hold the widget to remove it, then add it again.
