Daystitch Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

1. Information we collect

The developer of Daystitch (“the App”) does not collect or store any personal information about you.

The only exception: if you choose an external AI (ChatGPT or Gemini) in Settings, the content needed for an AI feature is sent directly to OpenAI or Google using your own API key when you use that feature (see “4. Third-party services”). It never passes through the developer’s servers.

2. Data the App handles and where it is stored

The following data is stored only on your device and in your own iCloud (if iCloud sync is on). The developer cannot view or access it.

  • Events, to-dos, habits, notes (handwriting, photos, text, stickers and moods) and templates
  • Handwriting recognition results (processed on your device with Apple’s Vision framework)
  • Audio recordings and their transcripts, summaries and minutes (audio is stored on your device; transcription happens on your device by default)
  • AI assistant processing (by default handled by the on-device Apple Intelligence model, and nothing leaves your device; see section 4 if you choose an external AI)
  • API keys for external AI (stored only in your device’s Keychain; never sent to iCloud or to the developer)
  • Settings such as themes and enabled features (on your device only)

iCloud sync uses Apple’s CloudKit to store data in your private area, shared only among devices signed in with the same Apple Account. You can turn it off in Settings.

3. Why the App asks for permissions

  • Calendars: to show events from the Calendar app inside the App (read only; nothing is sent out)
  • Location: to show the weather. Your location is sent to Apple’s WeatherKit to get the forecast. The developer never receives it
  • Notifications: for event and to-do reminders
  • Camera: to take photos for your notes (photos are stored only in your notes)
  • Microphone and speech recognition: to record meetings and other conversations on a notes page and transcribe them (recording starts only when you start it)
  • Add to Photos: to save a notes page to Photos as an image (only when you choose to)
  • Photo selection: to choose photos for your notes. The App uses Apple’s photo picker and receives only the photos you select (it does not access your whole library)

4. Third-party services

  • Apple WeatherKit: to get weather data (subject to Apple’s privacy policy)
  • Apple iCloud (CloudKit): to sync your data between your own devices
  • Apple Intelligence (Foundation Models): the default AI assistant. It runs on your device and does not send data to external AI services
  • OpenAI (ChatGPT) / Google (Gemini): used only if you choose one in Settings, agree, and enter your own API key. When you tap an AI button, the text needed for that task (events, to-dos, note text, recording transcripts, or the recording audio itself if you turn on “Transcribe recordings with external AI”) is sent. It is handled under each company’s privacy policy and API terms (on the free tier of the Gemini API, content may be used by Google to improve its services). You can switch back to Apple Intelligence and delete your API key at any time
  • OpenAI Privacy Policy: https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Apple Watch: your iPhone sends today’s and tomorrow’s events and today’s to-dos to your paired Apple Watch (device-to-device via Apple’s WatchConnectivity; nothing is sent to the developer)
  • App Store / StoreKit: to purchase and manage subscriptions (payment information is handled by Apple; the developer never receives it)
  • No advertising, analytics or tracking services are used

5. Deleting your data

Deleting the App removes the data on your device. Data synced to iCloud can be deleted in the Settings app under [your name] › iCloud › Manage Account Storage › Daystitch. Backup files are kept wherever you saved them.

6. Children’s privacy

The developer does not collect personal information. Using an external AI is optional; please follow the age requirements in each provider’s terms.
